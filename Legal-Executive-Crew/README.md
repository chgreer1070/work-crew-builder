# Legal Executive Crew

Open this folder itself in Cursor. Do not open the parent product repository when you want to use the crew.

The four helpers are:

- `/executive-intelligence` — Avery handles evidence packs, executive briefs, and legal-operations reporting.
- `/commercial-contracts` — Morgan handles contract economics and negotiation preparation.
- `/transactions-restructuring` — Jordan handles M&A and restructuring analysis.
- `/ai-transformation` — Riley handles AI transformation reporting and approved local code.

Final decisions, signatures, spending, external messages, and production changes stay with you. See `MYCREW.md` for the task list and `AGENTS.md` for the full rules of the crew.

Enterprise systems were not connected at setup. Give the crew exported files, and keep private contracts and deal files out of any public copy of this folder.
